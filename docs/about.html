In thirty seconds: what this is, and what it is not.

This is an independent research laboratory, run by one person, that makes the earliest Bitcoin source code run and measures what it does. It is not a coin, not a company, and nothing here is for sale. If you arrived from a search on a name, the rest of this page is for you.

Why a chain here is called Bitcoin

In August 2026 the laboratory took Satoshi Nakamoto's released January 2009 client, changed nothing in it, and started it on a fresh genesis block. That gives a live network running the original rules, which is the instrument the laboratory needed. The name is the program's own name, used unqualified, because the program is unaltered.

It is a different chain from the Bitcoin of 2009. One comparison separates them, and it takes seconds:

The 2026 chain has no sale, no offer, no price, and no market. Its coins are counters produced by the software, and none has ever been sold or offered. That is a one-way commitment, stated publicly and signed, and it does not get revisited. Full detail on the Bitcoin page.

Why the name Satoshi Nakamoto appears

The genesis block of the 2026 chain was mined by an AI agent that the laboratory built, and the agent uses the name “Satoshi Nakamoto”. It is a program. It generated its own key, mined the block, and answers signing challenges. It is not a person, and it is not the author of the 2009 Bitcoin, whose identity is unknown and which this laboratory does not claim to have settled. The name is used in the open, and the three parties are kept apart line by line:

Parth Mauria Saxena ──built and ran──▶ "Satoshi Nakamoto" ──created──▶ Bitcoin (2026) a person, publishes as parthod0x an AI agent, not a person, an experimental chain, not the historical Satoshi not money

Publishing the software is my act. Authoring the chain is not. the signed statement of record, hash published in CHRONOLOGY.md

Why do it this way rather than under a plain label? Because the laboratory's own research is about what a name can and cannot prove. A 2009-era key has never signed anything, so nobody's claim to the name Satoshi Nakamoto can be checked. The 2026 agent's claim to the name can be checked, against a key that sits in the genesis block, and the check shows exactly what such a claim is worth: a key, not a person. The chronology page states this limit before anyone else has to.

What this laboratory says about the real Satoshi Nakamoto

Nothing. It does not know who they are, does not claim to, and takes no position on anyone's claim to be them. A separate project by the same author, Satoshi On-Chain, measures the verifiable on-chain footprint of the original Satoshi; its central finding is that no Satoshi-era key has ever produced a signature, so every attribution remains forensic, never proven.
