Original Bitcoin Laboratory

The earliest Bitcoin, made to run.

Evidence‑first, executable reconstructions of the earliest Bitcoin — the 15 Nov 2008 pre‑release and the 3 Jan 2009 v0.1.0 client — built from two hash‑verified archives, with nothing disabled and no chain privileged. Released as candidates: a Bitcoin, not the Bitcoin. Not money.

And a third chain lives here — Bitcoin, which runs that same v0.1.0 client on a genesis of its own. Its own network, its own signed release, not a reconstruction and not interoperable with the two above.

What this is, honestly. A research microscope, not a coin. Which live network "is" Bitcoin has no factual answer — only convention; this lab privileges none. The experimental chains are stamped not money: no premine, no sale, no value assigned, no promises. The durable artifact isn't a running node — it's the reproducible recipe, which lets anyone re‑derive the exact genesis with no key and no node to trust.
